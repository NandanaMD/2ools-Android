2ools is a lightweight, privacy-focused collection of essential everyday Android utilities designed to work completely offline.

Instead of installing dozens of separate apps cluttered with ads, tracking SDKs, and network permissions, 2ools combines core utility tools into a single, clean Material 3 application.

Features included:

• Documents & PDF
- Images to PDF: Convert multiple photos to A4 PDF with rotation, reordering, and black & white scan filter.
- PDF to Images: Extract individual pages as high-resolution PNG or JPG images.

• Image Tools
- Image Compressor: Reduce photo file size with live preview and target size estimation.
- Image Resizer: Scale images by percentage or custom pixel dimensions with aspect ratio lock.
- Format Converter: Convert between JPG, PNG, and WebP formats.
- EXIF Inspector: View camera metadata, GPS tags, and strip embedded metadata.

• QR & Barcodes
- QR Scanner: Fast camera viewfinder and gallery image decoder.
- QR Generator: Create custom colored QR codes for URLs, plain text, and Wi-Fi networks.

• Calculators
- Percentage Calculator: Quick percentage, ratio, increase/decrease, and relative change calculations.
- Date & Age Calculator: Exact age in years, months, days, and birthday countdown.
- EMI Calculator: Loan repayment calculator with principal vs. interest breakdown.

• Unit Converters
- Offline conversions for Length, Mass, Area, Volume, and Temperature.

• Text & Security
- Text Inspector: Word, character, and syllable counter with reading and speaking time estimation.
- Case Converter: Transform text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case.
- Hash Generator: Generate cryptographic MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 hashes.
- Password Vault: Local encrypted password generator and credential vault using AES-256-GCM.

Privacy Guarantee:
- No advertisements.
- No analytics or telemetry.
- No tracking SDKs.
- Zero network permissions requested.
- All files and calculations are processed strictly on your device.
